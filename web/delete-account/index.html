Delete your Üisal account

You can delete your account in the app or request deletion by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Üisal and sign in.
  2. Open your profile.
  3. Select Delete the account and confirm.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address associated with your Üisal account. Include your account name so we can identify your account. Do not send your password.

Request account deletion

What happens to your data

Account deletion disables sign-in and clears your profile name, email, phone number, avatar reference, linked sign-in providers and password hash. Cloud backups are deleted, your posts and marketplace profiles are hidden, and device notification records are removed. Your internal account identifier and deletion record remain to preserve references in existing records. Other participants may retain information you previously shared. Contact us for details about retained information and retention periods.
