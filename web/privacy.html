Üisal privacy policy

26 November 2026 · eq

Üisal helps you draw house plans, share projects and communicate about construction work. This page describes the data used by those features.

Your account and projects

We process account identifiers, sign-in information, your profile name and contact details you provide to identify your account. Cloud and community features process the plans, photos, posts, comments, construction requests, quotations and messages you submit. Published content is visible to its audience. Messages and quotations are delivered to the people participating in the conversation or request.

Voice and device services

If you use voice input, Üisal uses your microphone and sends recorded speech for transcription. The transcript is used to interpret your request. Mobile services use device and app identifiers for notifications, usage analytics and crash diagnostics. Üisal uses Google Firebase services for these functions. If you choose a third-party sign-in provider, that provider processes your authentication.

Your choices

You can manage microphone and notification permissions in your device settings. Choose what you include in shared plans, posts and messages.

Delete your Üisal account

Open your profile and select “Delete the account”, then confirm. You can also request deletion by emailing estaidev@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Include your Üisal account name so we can identify it. Keep your password private.

Account deletion disables sign-in and clears the profile name, email, phone number, avatar reference, linked sign-in providers and password hash. The internal account identifier and deletion record remain so existing records can still reference the deleted account. Other participants may retain copies of information you previously shared.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact eq at estaidev@gmail.com.
